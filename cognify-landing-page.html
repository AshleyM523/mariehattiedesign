Frequently Asked Questions

What file formats are supported?+

Cognify supports PDF, DOCX, XLSX, CSV, images (PNG/JPG), and direct API/JSON payloads out of the box.

How accurate is the AI extraction?+

Our models average 99.8% field-level extraction accuracy across mixed, real-world document sets.

Is my data secure and compliant?+

Yes — Cognify is SOC 2 Type II certified with encryption in transit and at rest, plus GDPR-ready data handling.

What if the AI makes an error?+

Low-confidence extractions are flagged for human-in-the-loop review before they sync downstream.

How fast can I integrate?+

Most teams are live within a day using our API, native connectors, or no-code Zapier integration.

Is there a free trial?+

Yes — every paid plan starts with a 14-day free trial, no credit card required.
